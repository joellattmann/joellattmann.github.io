01 — R package

gganimslide

Reveal the layers of a saved ggplot step by step in PowerPoint or LaTeX beamer. ggppt() writes a slide with editable vector shapes and native animations; ggtex() writes a multi-page PDF plus beamer overlay code. Axes and layers never shift between steps.

v0.2.0 · MIT
